The Ætheric Tongue

The language of Ætherfall — known as Velskar — is spoken by the Renn (the awakened ones) and written in a script derived from the patterns of falling aether-dust. Below is a small reference grammar and vocabulary. Edit freely to match your constructed language.

Phonology

Velskar has 14 consonants and 5 vowels. It favors open syllables (CV, CVC) and has a stress accent on the first syllable of each root. The phoneme /ʃ/ (sh) is common in ritual words.

Common Phrases

English Velskar Literal Meaning
Hello / Greetings Vel-sha "Aether be with you"
Goodbye / Farewell Ren-tok "Walk in the veil"
Thank you Sha-vel "You honor the aether"
I understand Ves-ka "I see the pattern"
Danger Kor-ash "Veil tears"

Grammar Notes

  • Word order: Subject–Object–Verb (SOV). Example: Ren sha vel — "Ren honors the aether."
  • Plurality: Marked by the suffix -enn (e.g., ren "one person" → ren-enn "people").
  • Tense: Past is indicated by the prefix to-, future by we-.
  • Honorifics: The suffix -il denotes respect (e.g., Ren-il "Honored One").

Sample Text

Vel-sha to-ren il. We-sha vel-enn. Kor-ash to-ves.
"The honored one came. The aether will be honored. The veil-tear was seen."

Regions of Ætherfall

  • The Ashfall Marches
    Northern plains where aether-dust rains like snow. Home to the Dustcallers.
  • The Sunken Caldera
    A massive volcanic crater now filled with bioluminescent flora. The Calderan people live along its rim.
  • The Veilscar
    A permanent tear in reality running east to west. No one who enters has returned unchanged.
  • The Pale Coast
    Chalk-white cliffs and bone-sand beaches. Rumored to be the landing place of the First Arrivals.

Entities & Races

The beings of Ætherfall are shaped by the world's fractured aether. Below are the major races and notable creatures. Edit or expand this list freely.

Major Races

  • The Renn (Awakened)
    Humanoid, pale-skinned, with faint aether-lines glowing beneath their skin. They are the primary inhabitants of the Ashfall Marches. Height: 1.6–1.9m. Lifespan: ~120 years. They revere the Veil as sacred.
  • The Calderan
    Stocky, dark-skinned people who live around the Sunken Caldera. They have heat-resistant skin and excellent night vision. Height: 1.4–1.7m. Lifespan: ~80 years. Known for their metalworking and geomancy.
  • The Dustcallers
    Nomadic mystics who wander the Ashfall Marches. They communicate with the aether-dust and can "read" the patterns of falling particles to predict weather, danger, and events. Height: 1.5–1.8m. Lifespan: unknown.
  • The Veil-Wraiths
    Half-corporeal entities that exist on the edge of the Veilscar. Neither fully alive nor dead, they are drawn to strong aetheric disturbances. They cannot be touched, but can be heard whispering in Velskar.

Notable Creatures

  • Aether-Moth
    Large, translucent-winged moth that feeds on concentrated aether. Its wings glow faintly at night. They are considered good luck by the Renn.
  • Korath (Veil-Hound)
    Wolf-like predator with six legs and a hide that shifts between solid and vapor. They hunt in the Veilscar and along its edges. Extremely dangerous.
  • Elder Mycelium
    A massive underground fungal network that spans the entire continent. Some believe it is sentient. The Calderan use its fibers for building and its spores for medicine.

History of Ætherfall

The timeline below covers the major eras of Ætherfall's history. Edit freely to match your world's story.

Timeline of the Shattered Realm

  • Pre-0 AV — The Age of Wholeness
    Ætherfall was one unified world. The Veil between reality and the aether was intact. No records survive from this age.
  • 0 AV — The Shattering
    A cataclysmic event tore the Veil open, creating the Veilscar. The aether flooded into the physical world, reshaping continents and awakening the first Renn. This event marks year zero of the Ætherfall calendar (AV = "After Veil").
  • 1–50 AV — The Age of Ashes
    The world was covered in aether-dust fallout. Survivors banded together in small communities. The first Dustcallers appeared, learning to read the dust-patterns.
  • 51–200 AV — The Calderan Emergence
    The Calderan people emerged from the Sunken Caldera, having adapted to life in the warm, bioluminescent environment. Trade routes opened between the Marches and the Caldera.
  • 201–350 AV — The Veil Wars
    Three major conflicts fought over control of the Veilscar and its aether-rich territories. The Renn and Calderan fought alongside each other against the Veil-Wraiths. The wars ended in an uneasy truce.
  • 351–500 AV — The Quiet Centuries
    A period of relative peace and rebuilding. The Elder Mycelium was discovered and cultivated. Written records become more reliable.
  • 501–600 AV — The Pale Migration
    Large numbers of Renn migrated to the Pale Coast, seeking new resources and fleeing growing instability in the Marches. Coastal settlements flourished.
  • 601 AV–Present — The Current Age
    The world is fragmented but stable. The Veilscar continues to expand slowly. New aetheric phenomena are emerging. The Dustcallers speak of a coming "Second Shattering."
